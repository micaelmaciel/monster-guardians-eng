Monster Guardians - original Japanese story script
===================================================

96 messages in game order: msg 00-09 (opening narration), msg 10-62 (main story), L00-L32 (ending).
L30-L32 are alternate versions of the same final scene.
Message numbers are only for alignment; they are not ROM addresses.
